Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 2026  ·  GoldBullsFX

1. Introduction

GoldBullsFX ("we", "our", or "us") provides a mobile and web application that displays XAUUSD trading signals. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and your rights regarding that information. By using GoldBullsFX, you agree to the practices described in this policy.

2. Information We Collect

We collect minimal information to operate the app:

  • Push notification token — if you allow notifications, OneSignal assigns your device an anonymous token so we can send you signal alerts. This token is not linked to your name, email, or any personal identity.
  • Usage data — basic, anonymous analytics such as page views and session counts, collected automatically by our hosting provider (Netlify). No personally identifiable information is included.
  • Live price data — the app fetches the current XAUUSD price from Twelve Data's API to display status information. Your IP address may be visible to Twelve Data as part of this request; we do not store it.

We do not collect:

  • Your name, email address, or phone number
  • Account login credentials (there is no account system)
  • Location data
  • Financial or payment information

3. How We Use Your Information

The information we collect is used solely to:

  • Deliver push notifications about new signals and trade updates
  • Display live XAUUSD price and signal status in the app
  • Monitor app performance and fix technical issues

We do not sell, rent, or share your information with third parties for marketing purposes.

4. Third-Party Services

GoldBullsFX uses the following third-party services, each with their own privacy policies:

  • Supabase — database hosting. Stores signal data only (no personal data). supabase.com/privacy

Signal data is retained indefinitely for historical reference. Anonymous push notification tokens are retained by OneSignal until you unsubscribe from notifications or uninstall the app. We do not retain any personal data because we do not collect it.
